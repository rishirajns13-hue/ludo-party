Three ways the whole game could feel

Each concept is a complete interface: home, room, your turn, chatting, and the win. They share the same board, pieces and dice, so you're comparing how the game works and feels, not costumes. Every concept comes in four colour palettes.

  • 1Get you into a game with Ammu in two taps.
  • 2Make it obvious whose turn it is, even at a glance.
  • 3Let you trash-talk without leaving the board.
  • 4Make bonks, sixes and wins feel big.
  • 5Keep the all-time score, because that's the real game.

Your picks, trimmed for less friction

Kept

Faces on the pieces, the fixed 3D dice, hop moves, bonk, home fireworks, near miss, the win dance, live chat with bubbles, the emote wheel, stickers, hold-to-talk, rooms for 2 to 4 with bots, arcade sounds, and 2 v 2 teams.

Made simpler

The profile page becomes the all-time score on the home screen. Customizing is just three things: your colour, your avatar and the palette, with no locker screen.

Left out for now

Truce deals, the waiting-room mini-game and instant replay. Each one adds a screen or a choice and isn't needed for a fun game with friends. Any of them can come back later.

Mockups for choosing a direction. Tap the dice, hold the mic, send a message or hold the board in concept 3. Nothing here is online.
