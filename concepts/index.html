T

Full themes

Six complete looks for the game screen. Each one changes the board, pieces, dice, fonts and background together. Tap the dice in any phone to roll it.

B

Board layouts

Different shapes of board, including ones built for 3 and 6 players. Any of them can wear any theme's colours.

P

Pieces

What you move around the board. Players could pick their own set, so two people never look the same.

D

Dice & rollers

Six ways to get a number from 1 to 6. Tap each one. D1 is the current dice with today's "invisible dice" bug fixed.

M

Motion lab

How pieces move and how big moments feel. Press ▶ Play or tap the strip. These can mix: hops for normal moves and a rocket on 6s, for example.

C

Talk & communicate

Live chat, plus ways to talk that don't need typing: pointing at tiles, drawing on the board, voice, stickers, moods and deals. All of them work here, so try them.

L

Lobby & players

Rooms for 2 to 6 players, player profiles, and something fun to do while you wait for friends.

X

Your locker

A customize screen where each player mixes their own look. Change anything on the left and the phone on the right updates live.

S

Sound packs

Every sound is generated in the browser, so there's nothing to download. Tap to hear each pack.

F

Fun extras

Bigger ideas without a mockup yet. Heart the ones you'd want and I'll design them properly.
